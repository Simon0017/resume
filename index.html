About Me

A dedicated and detail-oriented Certified Public Accountant with a strong academic foundation and practical experience across energy, electrical, and public-sector organisations in Kenya.

My journey began at Chuka University where I earned a Bachelor of Commerce (Accounting Option) with Second Class Upper Division honours — followed by the full KASNEB CPA qualification, completing all three parts by 2023. I combine technical accounting rigour with strong interpersonal skills, a growth mindset, and genuine enthusiasm for contributing to organisational financial health.

Whether reconciling accounts, supporting audit preparations, or analysing financial data, I bring accuracy, professionalism, and a commitment to continuous learning to every role.
